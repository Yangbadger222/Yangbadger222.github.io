ABOUT

Mechatronics & Robotic Systems student at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, interested in how robots understand where they are, decide where to go, and turn that decision into action.

My work crosses autonomous navigation, GNSS / RTK, SLAM, robot learning, loco-manipulation and real-world robotics. I like projects that move from an algorithm or sketch to hardware that actually has to work.
